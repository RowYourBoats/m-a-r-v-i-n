Discovery was a proposed new retail format — a store concept built around a different relationship between customer, product, and space than Verizon's existing retail. The proposal didn't land. But the work built trust in our team's ability to think at the format level, and it's what made the Store of the Future proposal possible a year later. Discovery was the precursor.
