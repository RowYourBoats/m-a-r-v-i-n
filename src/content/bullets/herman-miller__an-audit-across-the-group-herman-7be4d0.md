## Medium

Defined product photography standards with Herman Miller photographer Mykah Stuk.

## Large

An audit across the Group (Herman Miller, Geiger, Nemschoff, DWR, and others) mapped what each brand produced, where processes diverged, and where they could consolidate without flattening what made each distinct. The output was a shared asset and file-organization standard, naming conventions, folder structures, and workflows, so products from different brands could sit together coherently without any one team doing extra work to keep up. The framework extended to product photography standards, defined with Herman Miller photographer Mykah Stuk.
