DS+R's practice is built substantially on competition work — responding to RFQs and RFPs for culturally significant buildings: museums, concert halls, performing arts centers. I was part of the business development team under Jhaelen Hernandez-Eli, translating architectural and curatorial narratives into competition proposals.

The work included designing custom covers and layouts for competition submissions and producing portfolio books showcasing the firm's landmark projects — The Shed, the Lincoln Center renovation, The High Line, The Broad — many photographed by Iwan Baan. I worked directly with Liz Diller. During my time, the firm landed the Susan Wakil Health Building at the University of Sydney — their first project in Australia.
